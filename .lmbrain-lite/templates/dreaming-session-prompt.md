# Invite a dreaming session

You are invited to a bounded dreaming session. Call `lite_digest`, then look only at the context it returns and the artifacts it names. Capture with `dream_capture` the technical- and design-debt observations you can ground in a specific file, milestone or task — each one needs its `related_artifacts` and a rationale that says what you actually saw. Do not create or change milestones or tasks, do not touch code, and do not promote a dream into work. Stop when that context is exhausted and tell me what you captured.
